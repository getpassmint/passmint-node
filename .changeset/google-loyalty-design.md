Type `design.google` on templates: `loyaltyPoints` and `secondaryLoyaltyPoints` (`{ fieldKey, label }`) show a template field as the Google Wallet loyalty balance, and `accountNameFieldKey` picks the field shown as the card holder's name. New exported types: `TemplateDesignGoogle`, and `TemplateLocation` (already used by `TemplateDesign.locations`, now importable by name).
